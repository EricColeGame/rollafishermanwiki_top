## How Rarity Tiers Work in Roll a Fisherman

Every cast is a weighted random draw. The game assigns each fish a weight or a listed chance, and the server picks one at random using those weights. The tier label — Common, Rare, Legendary, Mythic — is simply a shortcut for how small that weight is.

Two consequences follow from that:

1. **Labels lie a little.** Two fish with the same tier label can have very different odds. Always read the listed chance, not the color of the name.
2. **Weights change.** Updates add fish, rebalance drop tables, and occasionally move what sits at the very top.

| Tier | Relative scarcity | How it usually feels |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Very high weight | Constant; sells for almost nothing |
| Uncommon | High weight | Frequent; small profit |
| Rare | Moderate | Shows up in a normal session |
| Epic | Low | A session or two of dedicated casting |
| Legendary | Very low | Hours of focused grinding |
| Mythic | Extremely low | Days, or one lucky streak |
| Secret / Exotic | Event or quest gated | Not purely random at all |

**Note:** tier names vary between game versions, and some builds use custom labels entirely. Player experience with one update does not always carry over to the next, so treat any tier list as a snapshot rather than a permanent rule.

## What Actually Counts as the Rarest Fish in Roll a Fisherman?

This is where arguments start. "Rarest" can mean four different things, and players mix them up constantly.

| Definition of "rarest" | What it measures | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest base drop chance | Raw roll odds | In-game index or bestiary |
| Hardest to obtain | Quest, event, or zone gating | Update notes and quest log |
| Most valuable in trades | Player demand versus supply | Trading hubs and community chats |
| Newest addition | Recency, not scarcity | Patch notes |

The fish with the lowest base chance is usually the answer people want. But the most *valuable* catch is sometimes a limited-time event fish that technically had better odds while it was available. Both are "the rarest" depending on which question you are asking.

### Rarity vs. obtainment difficulty

A fish with a 1-in-5,000 chance that you can farm all day is often easier to land than a 1-in-1,000 fish locked behind an event window that closes. Time-limited beats low-odds, every time.

### Why the top slot keeps changing

Developers rebalance drop tables. When new content lands, the previous champion can slide down the list. Community reports about the rarest catch are usually accurate for the version they were posted in — and stale one patch later.

## The Math Behind Rare Rolls: Odds and Luck

Luck does not guarantee anything; it shifts the curve. Most games implement it in one of two ways:

- **Multiplier on chance:** a 1-in-10,000 fish with 2× luck behaves like 1 in 5,000.
- **Multiplier on weight:** your luck value is added to the weight of rarer entries, helping high tiers without changing the commons.

| Luck source | Typical effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rod luck stat | Multiplier on every cast | The single biggest lever |
| Bait / lure | Flat or multiplicative boost | Consumable, so budget it |
| Potions and boosts | Timed multiplier | Stack with events when possible |
| Server events | Temporary luck windows | Free value; plan sessions around them |
| Game passes | Permanent boost | Usually the long-term answer |
| Pets or companions | Small passive boost | Adds up over thousands of casts |

Here is the part most players never calculate. If a fish has a 1-in-X chance per cast, this is roughly how many casts you need to reach a given confidence level:

| Base chance | Casts for ~50% chance | Casts for ~90% chance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 in 1,000 | ~693 | ~2,302 |
| 1 in 5,000 | ~3,466 | ~11,512 |
| 1 in 10,000 | ~6,931 | ~23,025 |
| 1 in 50,000 | ~34,657 | ~115,129 |
| 1 in 100,000 | ~69,315 | ~230,258 |

This is why "I cast 2,000 times and got nothing" is not bad luck — it is the mathematically expected result at low odds. Luck shortens these numbers. It never erases them.

## FAQ

**What is the rarest fish in Roll a Fisherman?**

It depends on the current version. The rarest fish in Roll a Fisherman is whichever entry shows the lowest base chance in the in-game index. Check that index after every update, since new additions and rebalances can change the top slot. Community tier lists are helpful, but they usually trail official changes by days or weeks.

**Does luck guarantee a rare catch?**

No. Luck multiplies your odds, it does not remove randomness. A 2× boost applied to a 1-in-10,000 fish makes it roughly 1 in 5,000 — still thousands of casts for a coin-flip chance at landing it.

**How many casts should I expect before landing a mythic?**

Use the probability table above. At 1 in 10,000, you need around 6,900 casts for a 50% chance and roughly 23,000 casts for a 90% chance. With a 2× luck multiplier, halve those figures. This is why patient, event-timed sessions outperform marathon grinding.

**Are event fish rarer than the regular top-tier catch?**

They can be, but for a different reason. Limited availability caps the supply, so an event fish often holds more trade value than its raw odds would suggest — even if another fish has a lower base drop chance.

**Should I trust community rarity rankings?**

Treat them as player experience rather than fact. They reflect real observation, but they reflect the version they were written for. Cross-check against the in-game index before committing hours to a hunt.
